How this is worked out

Each month adds income, subtracts every active company bill at its typical amount, subtracts recurring expenses, and moves transfers into their account. Accounts then grow by their own annual rate. It is a straight-line projection, not a promise — it is only as good as the typical amounts you keep up to date.
