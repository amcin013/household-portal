How it works

  1. Upload the file. Nothing is written yet.
  2. It finds the header row and guesses which column holds the company name, the amount, the due day, and so on.
  3. You fix any wrong guesses and pick where the rows should land — bills, accounts, or recurring items.
  4. Confirm, and it imports.

Matching is by name, so importing the same sheet twice updates the existing entries rather than duplicating them.

Going the other way, export a workbook with bills, accounts, recurring items, and full payment history on separate sheets.
