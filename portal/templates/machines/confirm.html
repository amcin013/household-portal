Force off {{ domain.name }}?

This is the equivalent of pulling the power cord. The guest gets no warning, anything it had not written to disk is lost, and the filesystem inside it may need a repair on the next boot.

Shut down asks the guest to stop properly and is almost always what you want. Force off is for a machine that has stopped answering.
